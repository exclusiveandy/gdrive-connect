Privacy Policy

Drive File Converter · Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Drive File Converter (“the Application,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) accesses and handles information when you authorize it to work with Google Drive. The Application automates file conversion between a designated Google Drive source folder and a designated destination folder through a GitHub Actions workflow.

2. Information and Google user data we access

Depending on the permissions configured for the Application and granted by you, we may access:

  • Google Drive file content: the contents of files selected for conversion.
  • File metadata: information needed to identify and process files, such as file names, MIME types, file IDs, and folder locations.
  • Destination folder information: information needed to save converted output files to the folder you designate.
  • Authorization data: OAuth tokens or equivalent credentials required to perform the authorized Drive operations.

We do not intentionally request access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other Google services for this file-conversion purpose. The actual permissions requested are determined by the OAuth scopes configured in the deployed Application.

3. How we use Google user data

We use accessed Google Drive data only to operate and maintain the file-conversion workflow, including to:

  • identify and retrieve eligible files from the configured source folder;
  • read file contents and convert them into the configured output format;
  • create or upload converted files to the configured destination folder; and
  • diagnose workflow failures and maintain the reliability and security of the service.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, targeted marketing, or unrelated profiling.

4. Processing, storage, and sharing

File processing is performed by the Application's configured automation environment, which may include GitHub Actions and Google Drive. During a workflow run, source files and converted output may be temporarily present in the workflow runner's working environment. The project owner is responsible for configuring runner cleanup, logs, artifacts, and credential storage so that temporary copies and secrets are not retained longer than necessary.

Converted files are saved to the Google Drive destination folder selected by the project owner or user. We do not sell Google user data or share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Data may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly as needed to run the workflow. Those providers process data under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not authorize them to use Google user data for purposes unrelated to providing their services.

5. Data retention and deletion

Source files remain in your Google Drive unless the workflow is specifically configured to modify or delete them. Converted files remain in the destination folder until you or an authorized account user deletes them. Temporary working copies, workflow artifacts, and logs should be configured for the shortest retention period needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

You can revoke the Application's Google account access at any time through your Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized access, but does not automatically delete files already created in your Drive or copies retained in workflow logs or artifacts. To request deletion of data controlled by the project owner, contact us using the details below.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to this project. These should include restricting access to workflow secrets, limiting OAuth scopes, using access controls for repositories and workflow environments, avoiding sensitive file contents in logs, and removing temporary files when processing is complete. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You choose whether to authorize Google Drive access and which files or folders are configured for processing. You may revoke access through your Google Account, remove files from the configured source folder, or delete generated files from the destination folder. You may also contact us with questions or requests concerning data handled by the Application.

8. Children's privacy

The Application is a general-purpose file-conversion utility and is not designed to target children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Application.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Application's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.
